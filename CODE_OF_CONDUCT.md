# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Cinema contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, experience, or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and corrections.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.
