# Rights and Catalog Policy

Cinema's catalogue is only as trustworthy as the rights evidence behind every playable item. This policy is a release gate, not optional metadata.

## Eligible content

A title may become playable only when at least one of the following is documented:

1. It is in the public domain in the relevant territory, with evidence for that territory.
2. The rights holder has granted a license or explicit written permission covering the intended playback method and region.
3. An official provider's current terms explicitly permit Cinema's exact integration method (for example, an official embed or documented API).

A link being publicly reachable, free to watch in a browser, indexed by a search engine, or present in a third-party app does **not** establish permission to embed, mirror, cache, download, or redistribute it.

## Required rights record

Each playable asset must carry the rights holder / licensor and evidence reference; asset and provider identifiers; territory allowlist; start and end dates; allowed methods (official embed, direct playback, download, caching, or none); attribution; ad and monetization restrictions; subtitle / artwork rights; and a takedown / revocation state.

Unknown or conflicting information is treated as **not authorized**.

## Content that is not accepted

- Unauthorized scraping or extraction from streaming websites.
- DRM circumvention, account sharing, token theft, or bypassing geo / access controls.
- Re-hosted media without a redistribution license.
- Arbitrary third-party scripts or extension packs that inject undocumented streams.
- Artwork, subtitles, music, or metadata copied outside their license / terms.
- Any provider integration that cannot be audited or disabled quickly.

## Metadata and artwork

Metadata services have separate terms, attribution, caching, and branding rules. Metadata permission does not grant video rights. Artwork must be used only within the metadata provider's allowed display conditions. No API key is bundled in the Android app, browser bundle, or public repository.

## Review and takedowns

Every provider and asset enters a review queue. A rights reviewer records evidence and approval. If a grant expires, is challenged, or is revoked, playback is disabled immediately while the record is investigated. The catalogue may retain a non-playable title page only where metadata and artwork terms permit it.

## User reports

Reports must accept title, provider, territory, issue category, and evidence. A credible rights claim triggers an immediate safety hold on the affected asset pending review. Keep an audit trail of decisions and response times.

## Required interface labels

- **Verified free:** approved free playback for the viewer's territory.
- **Official provider:** external authorized destination; availability may change.
- **Not available here:** no authorized source for the selected territory.
- **Rights review:** temporarily disabled pending verification.

Never label a title "free" or "available" solely because an unverified URL works.
